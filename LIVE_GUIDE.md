# Live tab — quick guide

What each control does, for use standing on the sideline mid-match. For the rest of the app
(New Match, History, Summary), see `README.md`.

- **Start / Pause** — top-left. Start when the ref blows for kickoff.
- **Score & clock** — top-centre. Updates automatically from goals you record below.
- **Normal chance** — the big row right under the score. Use this for a typical shot; it's
  the one you'll tap most.
- **Pass / Intercept** — the diagonal panel. Tap your team's **Pass** every time they keep
  the ball. After any possession change (a tap, the Action Button, or Control Center), the
  button briefly turns into **Intercept** — tap it quickly if your team wins the ball back,
  before the window closes.
- **Easy / Hard rows** — use instead of Normal when a chance was clearly easier or harder
  than usual.
- **Set pieces** (Corner / Penalty / Free Kick) — tap the moment one is won. Still tap the
  Easy/Normal/Hard outcome separately when the resulting shot happens.
- **Undo last shot / Undo last pass** — fixes a mis-tap immediately, right under each
  section.
- **Hold to End** — top-right. **Press and hold it for a second** to finish the match and
  save it to History; the button fills as you hold, and letting go early cancels. A plain
  tap does nothing on purpose — it sits right beside Pause/Start, and a stray tap there
  used to end a match mid-game.

## Tips

- The Intercept window is short (about a second) — tap as soon as your team gets the ball
  back, don't wait to see what happens with it.
- Goal / On Target / Miss mean the same thing on every row — left button is always Goal,
  right is always Miss, regardless of Normal/Easy/Hard/Set piece.
- A block by a defender and a save by the keeper are different taps: the middle (yellow)
  button is for either, tap the row that matches what actually happened (Hard for a
  defender's block, Normal/Easy for a keeper save).
- Don't worry about substitutions, injuries, or a slow throw-in/corner/penalty restart —
  there's no special button for a stoppage. Just keep tapping normally once play resumes;
  a quiet gap of 25 seconds or more is automatically left out of the Summary tab's
  possession percentage, so a stoppage doesn't get wrongly credited to whoever had the ball
  when it started.
